About me

I’m Sam.

Former professional basketball player and qualified personal trainer. I started Pleszak Physical Health after working as an occupational therapy assistant and seeing how exercise helps people feel better. Over the past 5 years we have applied this to over 20 psychiatric settings.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

We offer bespoke wellness packages for wards, services and teams. 1:1 sessions, group work, diet support and staff wellbeing. Mental and physical health work in tandem.

The aim is to support the physical and mental wellbeing of service users and staff.
