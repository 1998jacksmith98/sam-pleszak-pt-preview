 About Us

 Founder Samuel Pleszak, ex professional basketball player and qualified personal trainer started with a simple observation when working as an occupational therapy assistant; “I know I feel better when I’m exercising” and the science backs it. 

 Over the past 5 years we have been refining this simple idea and have applied it to over 20 psychiatric settings. We look forward to continuing to make an impact.
