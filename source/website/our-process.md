 How We Work With You

 Our process allows us to meet the wants and requirements of a range hospitals with limited space and resources to utilise their space and their community to provide a wholistic approach to physical health.

 Work With Us 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 Our Approach

 Understand the needs and requirements for the ward, service users and MDT. From there we can find the right personal trainer for you.

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 Our Coaches

 Our personal trainers have monthly supervisions with our founder to discuss, quality, timetable and engagement.

 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 Our Goal

 Whether you need 1 session a week or a multiple ward and staff intervention we can help.

 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 Example

 Cygnet Harrow. – Male, 42 bed ward. Services: Acute and Complex mental health and Autism services.  

 We provide harrow with a personal trainer 2 days a week. With this Harrows service users receive – 1:1 session, off ward sessions, support around diet, health plans (for staff also!) 

 We are also involved in the inducting of Harrows new staff members – talking about the high priority Harrow put on exercise/physical health and how all staff members can help.  

 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 Example 2

 Cygnet Lodge Kenton. 15 bed. Female  

 Service – in North-West London, offers a flexible mental health rehabilitation service for women including High-Support Inpatient Rehabilitation (Level 2) and Community Rehabilitation (Level 1).  

 Cygnet Kenton Lodge receive a one a week session. In this session we aim to engage as many service users as possible, We attempt to vary these sessions and have completed sessions including, stretching and mobility, HIIT, Low impact circuit.
