- `PPH-Logo-Colour.svg` — official colour master (viewBox 748.22 × 364.69). Charcoal wordmark `#343531` and sage `#CCD0C1` on the stylised “H” / leaf and on “HEALTH”.
- `PPH-Logo-Colour.png` — 3200×1560 RGBA render of that SVG, transparent background (use on light grounds).
- `PPH-Logo-White.svg` — official white / sage version for dark grounds.
- `PPH-Logo-White.png` — 3200×1560 render of the white SVG, placed on the site contrast colour `#1F2527` so you can see it (this PNG is not transparent).
- `favicon-512.png` / `favicon-349.png` — official cropped favicon PNGs from the same site.

The mark is a “PPH” monogram (the H finished with a leaf) over the line **PLESZAK PHYSICAL HEALTH**.
